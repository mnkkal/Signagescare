2. End-to-End Walkthrough Scenario: "When a New Lead Arrives"


To illustrate how every role interacts across the CRM, let's walk through a real-world scenario from lead acquisition to cash collection:


Scenario: Onboarding "Titan Eyeplus" & Handling a Rooftop Power Surge Breakdown


[Day 1] NEW LEAD CONVERTED
├── Super Admin adds Client "Titan Eyeplus Retail Ltd" (GSTIN: 27AAACT2727Q1ZB).
├── Adds Store Branch: "Titan Eyeplus - Ramdaspeth" (Nagpur).
└── Registers Signage Asset: "AST-TEP-001" (Main Facade 3D Acrylic Backlit LED, 450W, Samsung LEDs / MeanWell Driver).

[Day 2] CLIENT STORE MANAGER ONBOARDING
├── Super Admin creates user: Kunal Joshi (kunal.titan@retail.com, Role: Store Manager).
└── Kunal logs in to crm.signagescare.com:
    ├── Dashboard immediately reflects his brand ("Titan Eyeplus Retail Ltd") and role badge.
    └── Multi-tenant isolation prevents him from seeing Lenskart or other client data.

[Day 3, 10:00 AM] STORE BREAKDOWN OCCURS
├── Heavy power surge trips the circuit breaker; main 3D logo completely dark.
├── Kunal opens the portal on mobile, uploads a defect photo, sets Priority: P1 - Critical, Billing: Chargeable.
└── Clicks "Submit Service Request" -> Ticket SC-NAG-0045 created in "Open" status.
    └── System triggers instant WhatsApp / Email alert to Operations.

[Day 3, 10:15 AM] TRIAGE & SKILL-BASED DISPATCH
├── Operations Manager (ops@signagecare.in) opens the Daily Dispatch Board (/dispatch).
├── Sees Ticket SC-NAG-0045 (Priority: P1, Location: Ramdaspeth).
├── Filters available technicians by skill tags: checks for "Electrical" and "24V SMPS".
└── Assigns ticket to certified technician Rohit S. with ETA: "Within 2 Hours".
    └── Ticket status shifts to "Assigned"; Rohit receives automated mobile dispatch alert.

[Day 3, 11:30 AM] SITE ARRIVAL & GPS CHECK-IN
├── Technician Rohit arrives at the Ramdaspeth store.
├── Opens ticket on his phone -> clicks "Get GPS" (Latitude: 21.1399, Longitude: 79.0766 logged).
└── Enters arrival note: "Arrived on site with diagnostic kit and replacement MeanWell 400W SMPS".

[Day 3, 11:35 AM] MANDATORY PRE-WORK SAFETY GATE
├── Rohit tries to update status to "In Progress".
├── SYSTEM HARD STOP: Action is blocked with error:
    "Pre-Work Safety Checklist (PPE & Electrical Isolation) must be completed before moving ticket to In Progress."
├── Rohit completes the safety protocol:
    ├── Checks "PPE Kit Verified" (Safety helmet, insulated gloves, rubber sole shoes).
    ├── Checks "Electrical Lockout / Isolation Confirmed" (Switches OFF store MCB isolator, verifies dead line with multimeter).
    └── Submits the safety checklist -> Gate unlocked!
└── Status now updates to "In Progress".

[Day 3, 12:15 PM] CHARGEABLE ESTIMATE & CLIENT APPROVAL
├── Rohit diagnoses two burnt 400W waterproof SMPS units due to 415V phase-to-neutral voltage surge.
├── Operations creates an itemized Quotation (/billing):
    ├── Item: 2x 400W Waterproof MeanWell SMPS Driver @ ₹3,000 = ₹6,000.
    ├── GST (18%): ₹1,080 -> Total: ₹7,080.
    └── Because Total (₹7,080) exceeds the ₹5,000 threshold, system auto-locks quotation in "Pending Approval".
├── Store Manager Kunal opens his portal -> reviews itemized quotation -> clicks "Approve Quotation".
└── Status updates to "Approved"; approval timestamp recorded for audit.

[Day 3, 01:00 PM] REPAIR, INVENTORY DEDUCTION & SIGN-OFF
├── Rohit replaces the burnt power supplies.
├── Selects "SMPS-12V-400W" (Qty: 2) from the spare parts dropdown -> clicks "Record Part Used".
    └── System automatically decrements 2 units from warehouse stock and logs ₹2,900 unit cost against the ticket.
├── Rohit switches on MCB: all letters illuminate brightly and uniformly.
├── Takes clear "After" photo and uploads evidence.
├── Records mandatory Root Cause: "SMPS / Driver Failure".
├── Check-Out: Logs 11.5 km travel distance and ₹140 vehicle expense.
└── Clicks "Check-Out" and marks ticket as "Completed".

[Day 3, 01:30 PM] CLIENT SERVICE RATING & QUALITY CHECK
├── Kunal receives completion alert on WhatsApp: "Work Completed for AST-TEP-001".
├── Opens ticket -> views Before photo (dark sign) vs. After photo (glowing sign).
└── Submits 5-Star Rating with feedback: "Store front illuminated perfectly before evening rush. Great job!"

[Day 3, 02:00 PM] STATUTORY GST INVOICING & PAYMENT
├── Finance Admin opens /billing -> clicks "Generate GST Invoice".
├── System issues Tax Invoice INV-2026-0045:
    ├── SAC Code: 998719 (Signage Maintenance & Repair).
    ├── Tax Breakdown: CGST 9% (₹540) + SGST 9% (₹540).
    └── Client GSTIN: 27AAACT2727Q1ZB.
├── Titan Finance transfers ₹7,080 via corporate UPI.
└── Accounts records receipt RCPT-2026-0045 -> Invoice marked "Paid" with balance due ₹0.

3. Dashboard Role Badges & Permissions Verification


Every user sees their identity and brand explicitly on both the top navigation bar and dashboard header:


1. Super Admin (`admin@signagecare.in`):

- Navbar display: `Director Operations | Role: Super Admin`

- Dashboard banner: Shows `Role: Super Admin`

- Access: Complete visibility across all stores, finances, backups, and user settings.

2. Operations Manager (`ops@signagecare.in`):

- Navbar display: `Operations Controller | Role: Operations Manager`

- Dashboard banner: Shows `Role: Operations Manager`

- Access: Operational dispatch board, inventory management, safety incidents, mass incident batching, and quotations.

3. Store Manager (Client) (`store.nagpur@lenskart.com` / `kunal.titan@retail.com`):

- Navbar display: `Kunal Joshi | Role: Store Manager • Titan Eyeplus Retail Ltd`

- Dashboard banner: Shows `Role: Store Manager` & `Titan Eyeplus Retail Ltd`

- Access: Multi-tenant scoped only to their brand's stores, signage assets, invoices, and service requests.

4. Field Technician (`rohit.tech@signagecare.in`):

- Navbar display: `Rohit S. | Role: Technician`

- Dashboard banner: Shows `Role: Technician`

- Access: Assigned tickets, GPS check-in/out, safety checklist verification, and part consumption.
